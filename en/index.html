My workflow

    Dashboard Detection

Holter server down in Cardiology

Situation
Holters record the patient's heart rhythm and send the study to a server that transfers it to the Cardiology PCs. If that server stops, the study cannot be completed and the patient cannot be seen.
What I do
I check the signals and transactions on the dashboard, validate with ping and ipconfig, open the ticket as P1 and escalate immediately to infrastructure.

What I do
I run the cache cleanup when needed so the system is not saturated, and escalate if usage keeps growing.
